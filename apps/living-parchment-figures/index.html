How it works

Sketch
Draw over the faint mannequin. A closed loop puffs into a rounded part, an open line becomes a limb, a tap makes a dot, a zigzag makes spikes. Draw on top of a part to add a bump, a face or a stripe. Mirror copies to the other side.
Parts
Swap heads, eyes, hair, hats, bodies, arms, legs, feet and extras. Parts and drawing mix freely.
Shape and Colors
Sliders stretch the whole body, drawing included. Pick a style, a palette variant or exact colours.
Motion
Every figure is rigged automatically: try Walk, Run, Jump, Wave and Sit, change the face, pose it by hand, or walk it around a world.
Export
A .glb opens in Blender with its armature, actions and shape keys. Also VRM, LOD sets, crowds, sprite sheets, GIFs and a share link.
