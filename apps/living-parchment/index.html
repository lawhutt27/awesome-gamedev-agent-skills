How to play

Draw with ink on the page and the paper rises into a little world.

Draw a closed loop
Raises a terraced hill. Loop again inside a hill to stack a higher terrace.
Loop with sharp corners
A square or triangle builds a stone ruin on a flat plateau.
Draw an open line
Carves a river. The water flows the way you drew it.
Tap, or a short flick
Plants trees, reeds, standing stones or lanterns. What grows depends on the biome and the ground.
Two fingers
Twist to turn the page, slide up or down to tilt. Pinch in for a flat map; pinch out to walk on the page.
Walking
Use the thumbstick (or W A S D) to walk and drag to look around. Tap still plants. Press Map or pinch in to fly back up.
Biomes
Moss (Mossy Ruin), Autumn (Autumn Ridge) and Moon (Moonlit Tide) change the light, the water and the paper cutouts.
Undo, Clear, Share, .glb
Undo steps back up to 10 strokes. Share saves the whole world into a link. .glb downloads a 3D model for other three.js projects.

Desktop: right-drag to turn, wheel to fold in and out, Ctrl+Z undo, 1 2 3 biomes, V walk, H help.
