Include

How to play

Draw with ink on the page and the paper rises into a little world.

Draw a closed loop
Raises a terraced hill. Loop again inside a hill to stack a higher terrace.
Loop with sharp corners
A triangle, square or jagged shape raises a rocky mountain with ridges and a snow cap.
Draw an open line
Carves a river. The water flows the way you drew it.
Tap, or a short flick
Plants trees, reeds, standing stones or lanterns. What grows depends on the biome and the ground.
Tools (left side)
Draw lets the shape decide. Hill, Mountain, River, Lake and Road make that thing from any stroke. Plant taps a cluster or drags a row. Sculpt raises, lowers or flattens the ground where you drag. Erase rubs the page back to bare paper. Move turns the camera with one finger.
Moving the camera
Two fingers: twist to turn, slide up or down to tilt, pinch to zoom. Keep pinching out to walk on the page; keep pinching in for a flat map. On a computer: right-drag turns and tilts, middle-drag (or Alt-drag) pans, the wheel zooms, and W A S D slides the map.
Walking
Use the thumbstick (or W A S D) to walk and drag to look around. Tap still plants. Press Map or pinch in to fly back up.
Biomes
Moss (Mossy Vale), Autumn (Autumn Ridge) and Moon (Moonlit Tide) change the light, the water and the paper cutouts.
Options
Brush size, what to plant, camera angles, the sun, and switches for outlines, contours, wind, water and sound.
Undo, Redo, Clear, Random
Undo steps back up to 10 strokes and Redo puts them back. Random draws a whole new world for you (Undo brings yours back).
Photo, Share, .glb
Photo saves a picture of the view. Share saves the whole world into a link. .glb opens Export for games: pick the map size in metres, the detail, water, rim and plants, and optionally a heightmap and masks for Unity or Godot terrain.

Keys: Q E turn, R F tilt, + − zoom, W A S D pan (or walk), 0 reset view, Ctrl+Z undo, Ctrl+Shift+Z redo, 1 2 3 biomes, V walk, O options, P photo, H help.
